- `defer`: only when the thread meets the criteria for deferring above. Say which, in a sentence. A disagreement alone isn't a reason: keep discussing it with `follow-up`.

When you accept a judgement call the implementer didn't flag as a decision, add the `decision` to your verdict yourself: `"decision": { "chose": "What was done.", "alternative": "The main option not taken." }`.

A thread marked **Final** has used up its exchanges and can't go back for more discussion. If you still disagree on one, decide it yourself, taking the more conservative side, and add a `decision`:

- `resolved` with a `decision` keeps the implementer's answer.
- `follow-up` with a `decision` is a ruling your way: say exactly what to change, and the implementer carries it out.
